# Admin pages: every current page, mapped

Every page of the live Nmesis admin, plus the showcase's small admin, mapped onto the approved Engine admin design (`design-approved/admin/`, 9 files). The Engine design wins (owner decision). The old page's behaviour is the feature list, not the look.

**Sources:**

- Nmesis admin `main` at `d23d313` (2026-11-19). Screens: `tests/ui/pages.mjs` (31 entries, of which 27 have the sidebar). Routes: `backend/server.js` and `backend/people.js`.
- Showcase `main` at `5039ce5` (`public/admin.html`, `api/admin/[action].js`).

**How to read the tables:**

- **Design.** The `design-approved/admin/` file the page maps to, and the tab or section inside it. "**No design yet**" is flagged and collected in §C.
- **Data.** Today's store → the target, in the schemas of [`data.md`](data.md).
- **Verdict:**
  - **rebuild**: a new page in the Engine design over the same data.
  - **reuse**: the logic or rendering ports with little change, usually into an edge function or a package.
  - **drop**: not ported, because it is replaced or obsolete.
- **Size:**
  - **S**: part of a PR, under a day.
  - **M**: most of one PR.
  - **L**: two or three PRs.
- **Decisions** are cited as **D*n*** ([`decisions.md`](decisions.md)).
- **Roles today** use Nmesis's five staff roles over ten areas (owner, ops, sales, finance, viewer). Target roles follow D7.
