Infrastructure Research · Vendor Decision Report

Pixel-streaming hosting for the configurator.

Six hosting options for the Unreal-based automotive configurator, compared on verified pricing, MENA latency, session APIs, and operational burden — with a staged recommendation for the Egypt launch, Gulf expansion, and the self-hosting threshold.

00

TL;DR

  • Stay on StreamPixel for the 45-day Egypt launch. Its flat €99/month-per-project model (2 concurrent users included, unlimited streaming minutes, +€45/CCU to scale) on RTX 6000 Ada GPUs in its Germany zone is the cheapest and lowest-effort option for Egypt, where internet routing naturally favors Frankfurt/Europe. It is the price leader at every one of the three concurrency scenarios once utilization is moderate.
  • For Gulf (KSA/UAE) expansion, move to Vagon Streams. It is the only managed vendor with in-region GPUs (Bahrain and Dubai datacenters), sub-5-second "Instant Connection," a real REST API for per-session start/assign metering, and iframe + JS SDK two-way messaging. AWS GameLift Streams has no Middle East region at all.
  • Self-hosting and GameLift Streams are not cost-justified below roughly 50 sustained concurrent streams. StreamPixel's blended ~$48/CCU/month is far below the ~$480+/month cost of one always-on AWS GPU. Self-host only when you need Gulf data residency, 4K, full IP control, or per-session margin control — not to save money at small scale.
01

Key findings

  1. StreamPixel is dramatically underpriced per concurrent slot. €99/month per project includes 2 CCU and unlimited minutes; extra concurrency is €45/CCU/month. Because minutes are unlimited, it wins on cost as soon as each concurrent slot streams more than ~1,390 minutes/month.
  2. Vagon Streams is the per-minute alternative and the MENA-latency play. Billed per streamed minute by GPU tier plus a $0.67/day app-maintenance fee; Bahrain and Dubai are in its region list. Best when utilization per slot is low (spiky traffic).
  3. AWS GameLift Streams caps at a 1080p/60 pixel budget and has no ME region; its billing is per allocated capacity-hour (including idle/warm buffer), which punishes spiky consumer traffic. (A July 2026 AWS update adds custom aspect ratios from 320 to 4096 pixels per dimension, but still within a 1080p total pixel budget, so the 1080p ceiling holds.)
  4. Self-hosting on EC2 gives you Bahrain (g4dn) and UAE (g5) GPUs but imposes a real solo-engineer ops burden (signalling server, matchmaker/orchestrator, autoscaling, TURN, monitoring).
  5. Egypt's routing genuinely favors Europe. Egypt hosts roughly 16–20 international subsea cables that make a ~100-mile land crossing and break out into the Mediterranean toward Europe; an estimated 17% of the world's internet traffic transits Egypt (CSIS puts Egypt's share of global connectivity at 17–30%). A Frankfurt/Germany zone is therefore the right launch target for Cairo users.
02

StreamPixel Incumbent

  • Pricing (verified, streampixel.io/pricing): €99/month per project, flat. Includes 2 concurrent users and unlimited streaming minutes; scale at €45/month per additional CCU. Operated by Vardexa OÜ (Estonia), "by InfinityVoid." 2-day free trial, no credit card.
  • Hardware: NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada (48 GB VRAM, ~RTX 4090-class), 24-core CPUs — strong for a graphics-heavy automotive scene. Dedicated GPU per session.
  • Regions/zones: India (Asia-Pacific), Germany (Europe), USA (America). No Gulf region. Germany zone serves Egypt well.
  • Embed & API: iframe embed with postMessage/JSON messaging; Web SDK for a custom JS player; REST API with webhooks for CI/CD; real-time analytics dashboard tracking usage/sessions.
  • Cold start: "Instant startup, no cold starts."
  • UE support: Any Unreal Engine build with the Pixel Streaming plugin (UE5). 4K streaming included.
  • SLA/contract: No published uptime SLA (flag). Month-to-month implied. White-label/enterprise via contact.
03

Vagon Streams

  • Pricing: Pay-per-minute by GPU performance tier, plus application maintenance cost of $0.67/day (~$20/month per app) and outbound data transfer at $1.50 per additional 10 GB after the first 10 GB/month. Exact per-minute Streams rates sit behind the calculator; third-party listings cite roughly $0.025/min (T4 "Starter") to $0.035/min (T4 "Pro"), with RTX tiers higher. (Flag: exact Streams per-minute unverified from primary source.)
  • Regions (verified region-prices page): 20+ datacenters including Bahrain and Dubai (Middle East), Frankfurt, Mumbai, plus US/EU/APAC. Bahrain and Dubai make Vagon the standout for Gulf latency.
  • Embed & API: iframe with <iframe id="vagonFrame" …>; JS SDK (window.Vagon.* methods: resolution match, focus, quality set, session info); Unreal Engine SDK over WebSocket; REST API (/streams/{id}/start-machine, /assign-machine) for per-visitor session assignment and metering; visitor-stats analytics; budget limits; white-labeling as a premium feature.
  • Queueing/capacity: Capacity methods (Cost Optimized / Balanced / Availability Optimized); "Instant Connection" keeps warm server pools for sub-5-second starts across regions including MENA; "Instant Installation" removes app-install time.
  • UE support: Unreal + Unity + any Windows desktop app; up to 4K/60.
04

Eagle 3D Streaming

  • Pricing (verified): Free trial (60 lifetime minutes, 1 stream). Core: $29/month, 300 minutes included, 10 GB storage, up to 10 concurrent streams; overage $0.10/additional minute (minutes reset monthly, no rollover). Enterprise: custom, up to 1,000 concurrent streams, 4K, analytics, SSO, self-hosted options.
  • Regions: Primary datacenters in US (Texas) and EU for self-serve; can deploy on GCP/AWS/Azure for enterprise. No dedicated Gulf POP.
  • Embed & metering: Stream-times analytics with per-device/location CSV export; project versioning; control panel with minute caps.
  • Concurrency: Self-serve capped at 15 CCU; beyond that requires enterprise. Epic MegaGrant + AWS Imagine Grant recipient; explicitly serves automotive/configurator use cases.
05

Arcware Cloud

  • Pricing (verified arcware.com/pricing): 15-day free trial (60 min, 2 CCS). Lite €10/month, €0.15/min, 1 project, 2 CCS, 1080p. Core €89/month, ≤€0.10/min, 5–15 projects, 3–10 CCS, 1080p (calculator shows €89 + €0.10/min; e.g., 500 min = €139). Custom: ≤€0.08/min, unlimited projects/CCS, up to 4K (3840×2160). "Cost per minute decreases as you stream more."
  • Servers: Munich, Germany and Los Angeles, USA. No Gulf region. Munich serves Egypt reasonably.
  • Embed & API: Web SDK (@arcware-cloud/pixelstreaming-websdk on npm) for custom HTML frontends; analytics (connections, viewing time, time-till-ready). Notice period 15 days (Lite/Core), 60 days (Custom).
06

AWS Amazon GameLift Streams

  • Billing model (verified aws pricing page): Charged per allocated stream-capacity-second (always-on + on-demand + target-idle warm buffer), regardless of whether actively streaming, plus per-GB-month content storage. This model penalizes spiky consumer traffic because instant-start requires paid idle capacity.
  • Verified stream-class rates: gen4n_high (multi-tenant, T4) $0.4982/hr Oregon, $0.6571/hr Frankfurt; gen6n_ultra_win2022 $1.82/hr Oregon; gen6n_small $0.16/hr Ohio. Single-tenant Windows classes recommended for heavy Unreal (gen5n_win2022 runs on a g5.2xlarge/A10G) have no publicly published hourly rate — only obtainable via the AWS Pricing Calculator/console (flag as unverified).
  • Regions (12, verified): us-east-1, us-east-2, us-west-2, Mumbai, Tokyo, Seoul, Sydney, Frankfurt, Ireland, London, Stockholm, São Paulo. No Middle East region — nearest are Frankfurt and Mumbai.
  • Resolution: up to 1080p/60fps total pixel budget (a real ceiling vs 4K options).
  • UE support: UE 4.27 and UE5 up through 5.5 (Windows gen5n; DirectX 11/12).
  • Embed/API: WebRTC Web SDK, full session-management APIs, AWS CLI. Most control but most integration effort.
07

Self-hosted Unreal Pixel Streaming on EC2

  • Verified GPU on-demand (Linux) rates: g4dn.xlarge (T4) $0.645/hr Bahrain (me-south-1), $0.658/hr Frankfurt; g5.xlarge (A10G) $1.234/hr UAE (me-central-1), $1.258/hr Frankfurt; g5.2xlarge $1.4867/hr UAE. g5 is not offered in Bahrain; UAE (me-central-1) is the Gulf home for A10G.
  • Stack required: Unreal build + signalling/web server + TURN (CoTURN) + matchmaker/orchestrator + frontend. Options: Epic's reference Cirrus + Matchmaker (starter only, not scale-ready), the aws-samples/pixel-streaming-at-scale framework (SQS FIFO queue, Lambda poller, autoscaling), or TensorWorks' Scalable Pixel Streaming framework.
  • Effective per-stream-hour: ~$0.66 (Frankfurt g4dn) or ~$1.26 (Frankfurt g5) compute + ~$0.40–0.50 egress (≈4.5 GB/hr at 10 Mbps 1080p, egress ~$0.09–0.11/GB) ≈ $1.10–1.75/stream-hour, before ops.
  • Ops burden: High for a solo engineer — provisioning, autoscaling, elastic IPs, cold starts, TURN, monitoring, security, no vendor SLA.

Other credible providers

  • PureWeb (Reality): Fully managed, enterprise-grade, UE5 Lumen/Nanite support, 2-second launch, SOC 2, explicitly markets automotive car configurators. Annual/quotation pricing only (no public per-minute) — enterprise budget, less startup-friendly. No public Gulf POP claim.
  • Others (Arcane Mirage, and various niche providers) exist but are less proven for MENA; keep out of shortlist.

Reading the table

  • StreamPixel is the price leader at (b) and (c) and essentially tied with Vagon at (a). Its flat per-CCU/unlimited-minutes model crushes per-minute vendors once slots are well-utilized.
  • GameLift/self-host "on-demand" columns look cheap but assume near-perfect utilization with no warm buffer — unrealistic for instant-start consumer sessions. The "always-on" columns are the honest comparison for a good UX, and there StreamPixel is 4–10× cheaper.

Below that (very spiky, low usage), per-minute Vagon wins; above it, StreamPixel wins. Scenario (a) sits near the line (~1,000 min/CCU → Vagon marginally cheaper); (b) and (c) are well above it → StreamPixel.

11

MENA latency

  • Egypt → Frankfurt/Europe is the correct path: roughly 16–20 regional subsea cables transit Egypt over a ~100-mile land crossing and break out into the Mediterranean toward Europe, and Telecom Egypt — the country's dominant telecom and one of MENA's largest subsea operators, involved in 13+ subsea cables — controls that corridor. Gulf-routed hosting would send Cairo traffic eastward against the grain. So StreamPixel (Germany), Vagon (Frankfurt), or Arcware (Munich) are all good for the Egypt launch.
  • Gulf (KSA/UAE): Only Vagon (Bahrain, Dubai) and self-host EC2 (Bahrain me-south-1, UAE me-central-1) put GPUs physically in-region. StreamPixel/Eagle/Arcware/GameLift would serve the Gulf from Europe (higher RTT). This is the single biggest reason to shift vendors for Gulf expansion.
  • Resilience note: the Egypt corridor is a known single point of failure — in March 2024 four Red Sea cables were severed (traced to the Houthi-struck carrier Rubymar's dragging anchor damaging AAE-1, EIG and SEACOM), disrupting an estimated 25% of Asia–Europe–Africa traffic, with a further cut in September 2025. An in-region Gulf presence partially hedges this for KSA/UAE users.

Recommendations

Stage 1 — 45-day production launch, Egypt (now)

  • Stay on StreamPixel. Lowest cost at your expected concurrency, unlimited minutes (protects you from spiky traffic cost blowouts), RTX 6000 Ada is ample for a configurator, Germany zone is optimal for Cairo routing, and iframe + postMessage + REST/webhooks already cover your embed and per-session metering needs. Zero migration risk inside the 45-day window.
  • Provision CCUs to peak concurrency per brand (start at 2, add €45 slots as brands onboard). Instrument session start/end via the Web SDK/webhooks to meter cost-per-session.
  • Mitigate the SLA gap: get StreamPixel's uptime commitment and build-IP/data-protection terms in writing before signing a brand; keep a Vagon Frankfurt fallback configured.

Stage 2 — Gulf (KSA/UAE) expansion

  • Adopt Vagon Streams for Gulf traffic, routing KSA/UAE users to Bahrain/Dubai while Egypt stays on Frankfurt (either StreamPixel or Vagon Frankfurt). Vagon's assign-machine API gives you clean per-visitor session metering and queue control; budget limits cap per-brand spend.
  • Benchmark before committing: run a 2-week A/B of real RTT/QoE from Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai on Vagon Bahrain vs Dubai vs Frankfurt. If measured Gulf RTT on Frankfurt is acceptable (<~60–80 ms), you may keep everything on one vendor and skip the Gulf migration.
  • Watch the per-minute meter: if any Gulf brand's per-slot utilization exceeds ~1,400 min/month, price a StreamPixel-style flat-CCU arrangement or Vagon volume terms.

Stage 3 — When to self-host or use GameLift Streams

  • Threshold: economically rational only at roughly 50+ sustained concurrent streams with high utilization and reserved-instance pricing, or when a driver other than cost forces it: (a) Gulf data residency/sovereignty, (b) 4K output (GameLift caps at a 1080p pixel budget), (c) full IP control over uploaded builds, or (d) per-session margin control at brand scale.
  • If triggered: self-host on EC2 UAE (me-central-1, g5) and Bahrain (me-south-1, g4dn) using the aws-samples/pixel-streaming-at-scale framework; budget for a dedicated DevOps hire — a solo engineer cannot safely run autoscaled 24/7 GPU streaming with an SLA. Prefer GameLift Streams over raw self-host only if you want AWS to own orchestration and can tolerate the 1080p budget and no ME region (Frankfurt/Mumbai).

Caveats

  • Vagon Streams exact per-minute rates are not published on a primary page; figures used (~$0.025–0.035/min) come from third-party listings and may understate RTX-tier/region costs. Confirm via Vagon's calculator/sales before modeling Gulf economics.
  • GameLift Streams gen5n Windows single-tenant hourly rates are not public — only via the AWS Pricing Calculator/console. The gen4n_high figures used are a floor; the class you'd actually want for a heavy Unreal automotive scene costs more.
  • GameLift/self-host "on-demand" cost columns assume high utilization; real instant-start UX requires paid warm/always-on capacity, materially raising true cost.
  • StreamPixel publishes no uptime SLA and its very low per-CCU pricing may reflect GPU oversubscription; validate stability under concurrent load and secure written SLA/data-protection terms.
  • EUR/USD converted at ~1.08; AWS prices exclude VAT and data-transfer beyond the GPU compute line; Gulf-region egress is higher than Europe.
  • Enterprise/Custom-tier costs for Eagle 3D and Arcware at scenario (c) are nominal placeholders pending direct quotes — both offer volume discounts that will materially lower the per-minute rate.
